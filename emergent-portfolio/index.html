05 60 sec · Narrative Pitch

Emergent Film Concept — Before the Coffee Cools

Logline

Two founders sketch an idea on a napkin in a busy café, and before their coffee even cools, they use Emergent to speak it into a live, working application.

Visual Approach

A fast-paced 60 second spot. Dynamic camera motion transitions seamlessly from a tactile, macro shot of the paper napkin to crisp, code-driven UI motion.

The Arc

Instead of the classic trope of "finding a developer," one founder simply opens their laptop and describes the napkin sketch in plain English. Through snappy cuts, we watch the interface assemble itself in real-time. They test it right at the table and share the live link with the waiter. A relatable, grounded demonstration of turning ideas into software in minutes.

Project Note: The assets presented above are independent technical explorations and R&D studies. They were engineered to test the absolute limits of current generative AI tools, workflows, and rapid turnaround times, rather than as published client deliverables.
